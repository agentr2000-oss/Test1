# More Slash Commands for the Planner

**Date:** 2026-03-25
**Category:** Creative

## One-liner

Build out more slash commands to make the idea-to-plan workflow faster, richer, and more useful.

## Notes

Right now we've got `/idea` and `/plan` — the basics. But there's a lot more this repo could do with a few extra skills:

- `/review` — Revisit old ideas, surface ones that haven't been planned yet, flag stale ones
- `/refine` — Take an existing idea or plan and tighten it up, add detail, or rethink parts
- `/brainstorm` — Riff on a topic and generate multiple idea files at once
- `/status` — Dashboard-style summary: how many ideas, how many plans, what's fresh, what's gathering dust
- `/link` — Connect related ideas together, build a web of related concepts
- `/pitch` — Turn a plan into a short elevator pitch or summary you could share with someone

## Why it's interesting

The `/idea` and `/plan` combo is solid for capture and first-pass planning, but the real value comes from being able to *work* with your ideas over time — revisit, connect, refine, and share them. More skills means less friction and more reasons to come back to the repo.
